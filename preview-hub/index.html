GTCFX website revamp · Round 2

Homepage drafts for review

Three design directions for the new gtcfx.com homepage, built in Next.js with Tailwind and Motion. Scroll each draft slowly to see the section animations, and use the language button in the header to switch between English and Arabic.

Draft 1 · Light

Clarity

White and soft-gray surfaces with navy and gold. Opens with an animated GTC Go app on a phone, then an arc-shaped market carousel and stacking platform cards.
