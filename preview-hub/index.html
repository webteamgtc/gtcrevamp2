Change log

14 Oct 2026 · Round 17
  • Company hero background replaced: the candlestick chart and the light-beam box are gone, so the hard edges on the left and right are gone too. Behind the hero there's now a modern gradient: navy, blue and gold glows drift slowly, a soft colour sheen turns very slowly, a fine grid fades out at every edge, and a glowing gold horizon arc sits behind the stats.
14 Oct 2026 · Round 16
  • Company menu opens the page: clicking "Company" in the top bar now opens the Company page, while hovering still shows the mega menu. On phones, tapping the "Company" heading inside the menu opens it too. In this review link, links between the homepage and the Company page now work.
  • Legal Information is a normal-height section again: it no longer pins or adds extra scroll. On desktop the jurisdiction still changes as the section moves up the screen. Clicking a tab takes over until you scroll away. On phones it switches automatically. The section is also more compact.
  • CTA spacing: the space above and below the call-to-action band is now equal.
14 Oct 2026 · Round 15
  • Hero trading animation: the skyline is replaced with a light trading chart. Soft candlesticks grow in, a glowing gold price line draws across them, the latest price pulses and a light beam sweeps over the chart.
  • Legal section shorter: the pinned scroll is less than half as long as before (about 2.3 screens instead of 3.8), and the card is more compact. It still changes jurisdiction as you scroll.
  • New support CTA (the split + hub design was rejected): a compact navy band with a gold hairline. The title and text sit on one side and the two account buttons on the other, with a quiet animated price line along the bottom.
14 Oct 2026 · Round 14
  • Company page in Arabic: every section is translated: the hero, Legal Information with all 5 entities, Awards, Leverage and its calculator, Trust promise, Help Center, Partner with Us and the support CTA. The page title and description for Google are translated too. Brand names, licence numbers and registered addresses stay in their original form.
  • Please ask a native Arabic speaker on your compliance team to check the five entity texts and the disclaimers before launch.
14 Oct 2026 · Round 13
  • Final styles locked in: Legal uses Spotlight tabs (it still moves to the next jurisdiction as you scroll on desktop), Awards uses 3D coverflow, Partner with Us uses Dark cards and the Support CTA uses Split + hub.
  • Page cleaned: the other styles and the Design options panel are gone, along with their unused text and code. That's 14 files removed and a lighter page.
  • Hero heading smaller: the "About GTCFX" heading now uses the same size as the section titles (42px on desktop, 28px on phones). This applies to every inner page that uses the page heading.
14 Oct 2026 · Round 12
  • Support CTA: new "Help desk" style (now the default; the other styles are still in Design options). On the left are the title and four topic tiles showing what support covers (Account, Platform, Payments, General service). On the right are two large action tiles, Open Live Account and Open Free Demo Account, each with a one-line subtitle and an arrow. The compliance wording now sits in its own fine-print strip at the bottom with an info icon, and the background has soft drifting glows.
  • Content adjusted: the long paragraph is now split into a short line ("Submit an enquiry or contact our support team. We can help with:"), the topic tiles and the fine print, which keeps your compliance wording unchanged. I dropped "using one of the channels above", because this page doesn't list contact channels above.
14 Oct 2026 · Round 11
  • Help Center redesigned on a white background as a bento layout with a live illustration in each card. Fast Response is the big card: a chat plays on a loop (question, typing dots, reply) with a ticking "24h" badge. Local Expertise shows a world map with pulsing local pins and a bouncing marker. Secure & Confidential shows a lock and shield inside rotating rings with encrypted dots streaming through. The View More button now sits under the description.
14 Oct 2026 · Round 10
  • Trust promise now sits on the gray background with new cards. Each card has a lit stage on top with a large E, S or G in the gradient and an icon tile that a gold dot circles. The title and text sit below. On hover the card lifts, a gold-to-navy outline appears and the line at the bottom stretches.
  • Help Center is now white, so two gray sections don't sit next to each other.
14 Oct 2026 · Round 9
  • Partner with Us: 3 styles (switch them in Design options). Dark cards (default): two tilting program cards on a dark band, each with its own live animation. Partners Program shows clients orbiting an IB hub with referrals flowing in; GTCFX Prime shows a moving liquidity depth chart. Switch panel: a toggle between the two programs that morphs a split panel, with a button and the same animations. Classic panels: the previous expanding panels.
  • Support CTA: 3 styles. Navy banner (default): drifting aurora glows, a rotating light ring, twinkles and the moving gold border, with gold and outline buttons. Split + hub: text on one side and an animated support hub on the other, with icons orbiting a headset. Classic rings: the previous design.
14 Oct 2026 · Round 8
  • Legal changes as you scroll: on desktop the Legal section now stays in place while you scroll, and each scroll step moves to the next jurisdiction (Australia → Mauritius → South Africa → Vanuatu → Cambodia), then the page carries on. The gold line fills as you scroll, and a small "Scroll" pill at the bottom has dots you can click to jump. This works in both the World map and Spotlight tabs styles. On phones and small screens it still switches automatically.
  • Awards coverflow shows 5 cards at once on desktop: the front card plus 2 on each side, turning further away in 3D. Tablets and phones show 3. The list repeats so the ring stays full until more awards are added.
14 Oct 2026 · Round 7
  • Legal and Awards each have 3 styles to choose from. On the Company page, use the Design options panel in the bottom corner to switch between them live.
  • Legal: World map (default): a dotted world map with live pins for all 5 jurisdictions and animated arcs linking them, plus a navy detail card that cycles automatically with a gold progress line. You can also click a pin, a flag or the arrows.
  • Legal: Spotlight tabs: flag tabs with a sliding highlight, and a big card with a floating flag that slides in the direction you move.
  • Awards: 3D coverflow (default): the front award is large and the others turn away in 3D. Drag, swipe, click or use the arrows; it plays automatically and pauses on hover.
  • Awards: Trophy stage: pick an award from the list and the trophy rises onto a lit pedestal with sparkles. It tilts in 3D as you move the mouse.
  • Classic: the previous designs are still there for comparison.
14 Oct 2026 · Round 6
  • New Company page (/company) in the Draft 4 style: dark hero with skyline and live-counting stats, Legal Information with all 5 regulated entities, an Awards carousel, Leverage Information with a working leverage example, Trust Promise (ESG), Help Center, Partner with Us with expanding panels, and a support call-to-action.
  • SEO built in: page title and description, canonical link, links to all 19 language versions, Open Graph, breadcrumb and organisation structured data. There's also a new sitemap.xml and a robots.txt that hides the drafts from Google.
  • Menu: Company → "Company Overview" opens /company, and About Us now points to /company/about-us.
  • The award images here are stand-ins cropped from your screenshot. The live site uses your files in public/awards.
14 Oct 2026 · Round 5
  • One heading system: every heading's size, weight, colour and gradient now comes from a set of classes in globals.css, with no sizes written on individual headings: heading-display (hero), heading-page (inner-page hero), heading-section (section titles), heading-title, heading-card, heading-small, heading-label and heading-accent (the gradient words).
  • Adding is-dark switches a heading to white with a gold gradient. Light sections use ink with the navy gradient.
  • All section titles are now the same size. Before, Accounts and Register were slightly larger than the others.
14 Oct 2026 · Round 4
  • Homepage section titles: removed the section numbers (02, 03, 05, 06…) and the small titles above the headings (Markets, Platforms, An account that fits you, Leadership, Here to help, Get Started). Only the main headings remain.
