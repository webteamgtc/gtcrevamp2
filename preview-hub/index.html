Change log

14 Oct 2026 · Round 3
  • Moving border on the mega menu and language band is now 1px.
  • Logo is bigger (44px tall on phones, 48px on desktop), with no white frame or box behind it.
  • The code uses your official S3 logos (white on dark, colour on light) on the live site. This review link still shows a local copy, because the review host blocks images from S3.
14 Oct 2026 · Round 2
  • Language picker in dark mode: it follows the header, so on the dark homepage it is dark (navy and gold), and on light pages it stays white. The phone bottom sheet follows too.
  • Language picker full width: it now opens as a band under the header, the same way as the mega menu, with 5 columns of languages and the page dimmed behind it.
  • Moving gradient border (gold to navy, like the register form) on the left, right and bottom of the open mega menu and the language band, with no border on top.
  • Opening the language picker closes any open mega menu, and the reverse.
14 Oct 2026 · Round 1
  • Mega menu in the earlier attached style, now with every item from the current site: icon tiles with short descriptions, gold section titles and a promo card per menu.
  • Markets menu shows live (demo) prices for EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, USDCHF, Gold and three indices.
  • Phone menu with drill-down: menus first, then their links, with a Back button.
  • Language picker with all 19 languages: search, region tabs and round flags. Arabic, Urdu and Pashto switch the layout to right-to-left.
  • CEO photo converted to WebP (1.36 MB → 77 KB) for faster loading.
